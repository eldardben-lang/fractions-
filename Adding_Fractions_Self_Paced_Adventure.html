Let's Learn

Finding a Common Denominator

⏱ ~4 min

List the multiples of each denominator until you find one they SHARE — that's your Least Common Denominator (LCD).

Multiples of 4

4, 8, 12, 16, 20...

Multiples of 6

6, 12, 18, 24...

The smallest shared multiple is 12 — so the LCD of 4 and 6 is 12!

Don't Forget to Simplify!

⏱ ~3 min

Always check if your answer can be reduced to lowest terms — divide the top AND bottom by the same number.

Both 6 and 8 can be divided by 2 — so 68 simplifies to 34

Bonus Challenge

Adding Mixed Numbers

⏱ ~5 min

A mixed number has a whole number AND a fraction, like 1 ¼. Here's how to add two of them:

1

Add the whole numbers first: 1 + 2 = 3

2

Find the LCD of the fraction parts (4 and 3): LCD = 12

3

Convert and add the fractions: 1/4 = 3/12, 1/3 = 4/12  →  3/12 + 4/12 = 7/12

4

Combine: 3 + 7/12 = 3 7/12

🦉Tricky bit: if the fractions add up to MORE than a whole (like 6/4), turn that into a mixed number and add the extra whole to your total!
